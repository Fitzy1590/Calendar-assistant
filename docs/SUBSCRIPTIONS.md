# Subscriptions

Left menu → **Subscriptions** → **Import a bank statement**.

* **Which files:** download the last 3 months or more from your bank's website or app as **CSV**, **OFX/QFX** or **QIF**. The format is detected from the file.
* **What it finds:** the same merchant charging at a steady rhythm (weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, yearly) for a steady amount. Well-known services (Netflix, Spotify, Microsoft 365, Telstra and others) need only one charge. One big charge from a known service is assumed to be a yearly plan.
* **Then:** groups by category with totals per month and per year, flags price rises, and shows "via Apple / Google Play / PayPal" when the charge goes through them.
* **How to cancel:** each row has plain steps and links to the provider's own account page (or the Apple, Google Play or PayPal subscriptions page, or a search when we don't know the page). Press **I've cancelled it** and the app warns you if a later statement shows another charge.
* **Not a subscription:** hides a false match; it stays hidden when you import again.
* **Privacy:** the file is read on this PC and never uploaded. Only the list of subscriptions found is kept (in your settings), not your transactions. The app never signs in to a company or cancels anything for you.
